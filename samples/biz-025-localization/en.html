THE CLASS

What happens in class

Shape nerikiri

Nerikiri are soft sweets made from sweet bean paste, shaped by hand into seasonal motifs like flowers and leaves.

Shop locally

A week before class you'll get a shopping list with substitutes you can find at most supermarkets outside Japan. We don't ship food.

Keep the recipe

After class you receive a recipe PDF with step photos, so you can make them again on your own.

From booking to class

FAQ

Questions before you book

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel at least 24 hours before your class (Japan time) for a full refund. After that, we can move you to another date once. (Example policy.)

I can't find some ingredients. Is that okay?

Yes. Your list includes substitutes. If you're unsure, reply to the email with a photo of what you found and we'll tell you if it works.

Is the class in English?

Yes. The teacher speaks slowly and shows each step on camera, so you can follow along even if English isn't your first language.
