Your stay

What to expect

01

A shared cedar bath

One bath for all guests, used in turns. A small sign on the door shows when it's free.

02

Breakfast, if you like

Rice, miso soup and seasonal side dishes, served at 8:00. Tell us about allergies when you book.

03

Quiet after 22:00

It's a residential street, so we ask guests to keep voices low at night. Earplugs are at the front desk.

All rooms use futon mattresses on tatami. We lay them out in the evening and fold them away in the morning.
