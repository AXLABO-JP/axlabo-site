The problem

Scheduling shouldn't eat your weekend

  • Availability in five places

    Messages, notes and a spreadsheet that only one person understands.

  • Last-minute swaps

    Someone can't make it, and the whole week has to be rebuilt by hand.

  • "Is that fair?"

    Weekend and closing shifts pile up on the same few people without anyone noticing.

Features

Everything a small team needs, nothing more

One link for availability

Staff tap the days they can work on their phone. No app to install.

Suggested schedule

A first draft that respects everyone's hours and spreads weekend shifts evenly.

Swaps without the chaos

Staff offer a shift, a teammate accepts, and you approve with one tap.

How it works

From availability to a published week

  1. STEP 1

    Share one link

    Your team adds the days and hours they can work.

  2. STEP 2

    Review the draft

    Adjust the suggested schedule by dragging shifts where you want them.

  3. STEP 3

    Publish

    Everyone gets their shifts by email, and changes stay in one place.
