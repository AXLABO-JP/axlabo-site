How to order

Three simple steps

  1. STEP 1

    Take a seat

    If a seat is free, sit anywhere. If we are full, write your name on the list by the door.

  2. STEP 2

    Point or tell us

    Show the dish on this page or on the paper menu. Staff will repeat your order to check.

  3. STEP 3

    Pay when you leave

    Bring your slip to the counter. There is no service charge and no tipping.
