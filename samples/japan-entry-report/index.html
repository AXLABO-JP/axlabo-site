Japan Entry Assessment

An example of the one-page report AXLABO prepares after a short hearing. It organizes the questions a company usually faces before setting up in Japan, and lists what to confirm with licensed professionals. It does not answer those questions for you.

0 · Brief

What the company told us

Only facts heard in the hearing are recorded here. We do not add assumptions about the market.

Company EXAMPLE

A small software company in Europe with six people. No entity or staff in Japan yet.

Goal in Japan EXAMPLE

Sell its scheduling software to small clinics and studios, starting with online sales.

People EXAMPLE

The founders live outside Japan. One founder may spend some months in Japan next year.

Open decisions EXAMPLE

Whether to set up an entity now or later, and whether a Japan address is needed at the start.

All company details on this page are fictional and shown only as an example of the report format.

1 · Issues

Questions to settle before setting up

Each item is a question, not an answer. Which option fits depends on your situation and must be confirmed with a licensed professional.

01

Form of presence

Options people commonly compare include selling from abroad without an entity, a branch, or a Japanese company (for example a KK or GK). The trade-offs for your case are not decided here.

Needs confirmation

02

Address in Japan

Whether you need a Japan address, and what kind of address is acceptable for your chosen form, varies by situation. Please do not rely on this sample for that answer.

Needs confirmation

03

Main procedures

Registration, tax-related notifications and opening a bank account are typically discussed. Order, timing and eligibility depend on the form you choose.

Needs confirmation

04

People and residence

If a founder plans to stay and work in Japan, residence status becomes a separate question. It is outside what this report can decide.

Needs confirmation

2 · Document checklist

Documents you may be asked for

An example list to help you prepare. Requirements vary — confirm the exact list with the professional who handles your procedure.

  • Registration documents of the parent companyIf you set up as a foreign company. Translation may be requested.
  • Identification of the representativesFor example passport copies.
  • Proof of address of the representativesFormat depends on the country of residence.
  • Signature or seal certificationWhich one is accepted varies. Confirm first.
  • Basic company informationPlanned name, business purpose, capital, officers.
  • Information about the Japan addressOnly if an address is required for your form.

This checklist is an example for a fictional company. It is not a complete or authoritative list.

3 · Questions for professionals

What to ask, and whom

You engage these professionals directly. AXLABO does not introduce or broker professionals.

TopicType of professionalQuestions to bring
Form & registrationJudicial scrivener (shiho-shoshi) or lawyerWhich form fits our plan? What is needed to register it, and in what order?
TaxTax accountant (zeirishi)What notifications are needed after setting up? How is selling from abroad treated compared with a Japanese entity?
Residence statusImmigration lawyer or gyoseishoshiIf a founder stays in Japan to work, what status could apply, and what does it require?
ContractsLawyerWhat should our terms with Japanese customers cover?
