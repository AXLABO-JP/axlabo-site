What we will not do

AI is useful, but it makes mistakes. So we keep a few firm rules.

  • We do not fill gaps in your information with guesses presented as facts.
  • We do not copy photos, text or reviews whose rights we have not confirmed.
  • We do not publish anything on your behalf before you approve it.
  • We do not guarantee traffic, bookings or sales.
  • Legal, tax and real estate transaction matters are left to licensed professionals.
