02 · Illustrative, not research data

One-page market hypothesis

These are starting assumptions to validate through real desk research and conversations. They are not findings, forecasts, or verified statements about the target market.

HYPOTHESIS 01

Start with specialty importers

Retailers seeking a low-disruption trial listing may be a clearer first segment than large chains planning a full category review.

HYPOTHESIS 02

Lead with a bounded pilot

A small, clearly scoped trial order may reduce the perceived risk of adopting an unfamiliar overseas food product and create concrete feedback.

HYPOTHESIS 03

Use a local channel lens

Specialty food importers and Japanese-goods distributors may be useful early partners because they can contextualize labeling, shelf placement, and buyer expectations.

Entry segmentSpecialty/imported-food retail channels
Value proposition to testSmall trial listing with limited inventory commitment
First proof soughtImporter feedback and one structured pilot conversation

Illustrative sample assumptions only. No market size, growth rate, adoption rate, or other invented statistic is presented. A real engagement would require source verification and client validation.

04 · Sample draft

Initial outreach email

A short first-touch draft focused on learning and fit—not an unsupported promise. Recipient, sender, company, and product are all fictional.

ToSample Target Alpha Import Ltd. — Fictional recipient
SubjectExploring a small trial listing for a Japanese instant soup line
Sample draft · fictional

Dear Sample Target Alpha team,

I'm writing on behalf of Sample Craft Foods Co., a Japanese manufacturer exploring market fit for our fictional SampleMiso Instant Soup Line outside Japan.

The product is designed as a simple shelf-stable retail item. At this stage, we are not assuming it is ready for your market. We are seeking practical feedback from specialty importers on labeling requirements, shelf placement, and the types of stores where a limited trial listing might be relevant.

Would you be open to a 20-minute introductory call? We can share a short product brief in advance and keep the discussion focused on whether further evaluation would make sense.

Kind regards,
Alex Sample
Sample Craft Foods Co.
