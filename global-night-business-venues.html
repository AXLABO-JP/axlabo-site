The information gap

Japanese travellers often can't find your venue details in Japanese.

For a traveller reading in Japanese, prices, dress codes, age and entry rules can be hard to find or understand across booking sites, social profiles and venue websites. When those details are unclear, they may not be sure your venue suits their plans.

Writing and keeping a Japanese page yourself takes time and translation help. AXLABO prepares one for you from the information you already publish or send us.

  • Practical details organised for travellers deciding before they visit
  • Japanese text written from the details your venue confirms

How it works

Listing rules

Who we list, and what we don't do.

Licensed venues only

We list venues that can show their current local operating licence (for example a business or liquor licence). We ask for a copy before publishing.

Not listed

  • Adult entertainment or sexual services
  • Escort or delivery-type services
  • Venues that cannot show a licence

How pages are written

No explicit content and no wording that reads like touting. Prices, opening hours and licence details are shown exactly as your venue confirms them.

Our role

AXLABO is a listing and information platform only. We do not take bookings or reservations, process payments for your venue, act as an intermediary, or charge commission. Age, dress code and entry rules are set by your venue and shown as you provide them. AXLABO does not judge or guarantee compliance with local laws; please confirm your own requirements with your local advisers. We make no promises about visitor numbers or revenue.

FAQ

Common questions

What does it cost?

US$200 per venue, one time, for producing your Japanese page. There is no monthly fee (US$0). Applicable taxes, if any, will be shown on the invoice.

Do you take bookings or charge commission?

No. AXLABO is a listing and information platform only. We do not take bookings, process payments for your venue or charge any commission. Travellers are directed to your own website or contact channel.

Which venues can be listed?

Venues that can show their current local operating licence, such as a business or liquor licence. We do not list adult entertainment or sexual services, escort or delivery-type services, or venues that cannot show a licence.
