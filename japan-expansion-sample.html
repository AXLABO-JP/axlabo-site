Sample Market Research Report — Table of Contents

An example structure showing how a Japan market research engagement could be organized for an international robotics startup.

i
SAMPLE — Illustrative Table of Contents Only

Nordholm Robotics AB is a fictional Swedish robotics startup created solely for this sample. It is not a real company or an AXLABO client, and this page does not describe an actual engagement or result.

  1. Executive Summary

    The report would bring together the working assumptions, major findings, decision points, and questions requiring further validation.

  2. Business Context & Entry Objectives

    The report would clarify the proposed business scope, target customers, entry objectives, and constraints supplied by the fictional case company.

  3. Market Sizing Approach & Competitive Landscape

    The report would define a research method for estimating relevant demand and mapping competitor categories without treating early assumptions as verified facts.

  4. Customer Segments & Buying Process

    The report would organize potential customer profiles, likely stakeholders, procurement considerations, and topics for primary research.

  5. Regulatory & Licensing Overview

    The report would identify regulatory areas and possible license questions to review with appropriately qualified professionals.

  6. Entity Structure Options

    The report would compare possible approaches to a Japan presence at a planning level and flag matters requiring licensed legal, tax, or registration advice.

  7. Visa & Residence Status Pathway

    The report would outline information to prepare for a discussion with a licensed professional, without predicting eligibility or approval.

  8. Office & Talent Market Snapshot

    The report would frame workspace, hiring, language, and operating considerations that may affect location and launch planning.

  9. Go-to-Market Localization Notes

    The report would organize questions around positioning, communication, sales pathways, customer support, and adaptation for the Japan market.

  10. Partner & Vendor Coordination Map

    The report would identify the types of external specialists and vendors that may be needed, together with responsibilities and unresolved dependencies.

  11. Indicative Cost & Timeline Framework

    The report would set out cost categories, sequencing assumptions, and timeline dependencies for later validation and individual quotation.

  12. Risks, Open Questions & Next Decisions

    The report would close with material uncertainties, validation tasks, decision owners, and suggested next conversations.

End of sample. This illustrative structure uses the fictional name Nordholm Robotics AB. It is not an actual client, case study, completed report, or statement of results.
